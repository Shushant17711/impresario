We take only what the bees can spare.

Every October the ivy honey stays in the hives. No sugar syrup, no heating, no blending with anyone else’s honey. If a colony has a bad summer, it gets the year off.

Four Holloways and roughly two million bees.

  • Ruth

    Keeps the hives. Has been stung 1,400-odd times and insists on counting.

  • Dev

    Builds the frames, drives the van, and runs the extractor at 5 a.m. so the kitchen smells of wax by breakfast.

  • Ivy, 11

    Names every queen. This year’s include Marigold, Duchess and Big Sandra.

  • Len, 84

    Started it all with two hives in 1987. Still checks the jars at the stand every morning.

The honesty stand

At our gate on Holloway Lane, every day from first light till dark. Jars on the shelf, tin on the post, card reader if you’ve no cash.
