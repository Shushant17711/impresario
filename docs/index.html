A skill that turns a coding agent into a creative director. It gets a subject and nothing else: no assets, no style, no follow-up questions. It invents a concept that belongs to that subject, builds it with choreographed motion and screenshots its own work before handing it over.

> the entire promptmake a website for a small family honey farm
Open the live site → Get the skill on GitHub Second test: Veda Computers

What the agent decided on its own

“The site where you scroll through a beekeeping year and watch the honey rise and darken, then pour into the three jars you can buy.”

Opening
A drop falls, splashes into a pool, and “Holloway” rises out of the honey.
The year
A live WebGL liquid fills the viewport and darkens from April to October. A Pfund gauge (the real honey-grading scale) tracks it.
Harvest
Honey floods the screen, then drains into three jars. The jars are the shop.
Close
A dripping wordmark. Click it and more honey drops into the pool.
