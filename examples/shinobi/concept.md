# Historical concept — not implementation evidence

See `README.md`: this is a mechanics-only negative example, not accepted creative work.

A single fight read as a sequence of stills. The page is a long take: the viewer
scrolls, the camera does not cut. Each section pins for one beat, the image
translates a fraction, a mask opens on a line of text, and the scene releases.

The fight is not shown. It is implied by what the camera refuses to show.
